Where should Wael and Z eat?

A proof of concept: a model trained on Wael's and Z's Beli rankings, reading how Yelp reviewers describe each place. It scores places neither of them has been to, explains why, and combines both tastes into one list. It rests on two people's lists, so the results show that the approach works, not that it is finished.
